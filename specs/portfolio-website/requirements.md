## 2. Functional requirements (EARS format)

### Navigation
- FR-1: WHEN the page loads, THE SYSTEM SHALL display a fixed/sticky top navbar with: name/logo,
  links to About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Certifications, Contact, and a "Download CV"
  button.
- FR-2: WHEN the user clicks a nav link, THE SYSTEM SHALL smooth-scroll to the matching section and
  update the URL hash.
- FR-3: WHILE the viewport is narrower than `md` (768px), THE SYSTEM SHALL collapse the navbar into
  a hamburger menu that opens a full-screen or slide-in mobile menu.
- FR-4: WHILE the user is scrolled past the hero, THE SYSTEM SHALL highlight the navbar link
  matching the section currently in view.

### Hero / intro
- FR-5: WHEN the site first loads, THE SYSTEM SHALL play a brief (≤ 2.5s) intro/loading animation
  before revealing the hero, and SHALL skip/shorten it on repeat visits within the same session
  (use sessionStorage flag, not localStorage-dependent business logic — see tech.md for any
  storage caveats in the build environment used).
- FR-6: THE hero SHALL display name, a rotating/typewriter line cycling through 2–3 of his role
  titles from section 1.1, a short one-line value statement, and two CTAs ("View Projects",
  "Contact Me").
- FR-7: THE hero SHALL contain a 3D animated scene (see `design.md`) that is interactive on
  pointer/mouse move on desktop, and auto-animates (no interaction required) on touch devices.

### About
- FR-8: THE about section SHALL render the bio from 1.2, plus a small fact-row (years of
  experience, location, languages, current education status).

### Skills
- FR-9: THE skills section SHALL render skills as an interactive 3D orbiting arrangement (desktop)
  with a fallback to a clean animated grid/list (mobile or reduced-motion).
- FR-10: WHEN a skill is hovered/tapped, THE SYSTEM SHALL visually highlight it and MAY filter the
  Projects section to show only projects tagged with that skill (nice-to-have, not blocking).

### Experience ("journey")
- FR-11: THE experience section SHALL present all 4 roles from 1.3 in reverse-chronological order
  as a scroll-driven visual journey/timeline (see `design.md` for the road/checkpoint concept),
  each with title, org, dates, location, and bullet points.
- FR-12: On low-performance-tier devices, FR-11's journey SHALL render as a static vertical
  timeline with simple fade/slide-in-on-scroll animation instead of the full 3D scene.

### Projects
- FR-13: THE projects section SHALL render all entries from 1.4 (minus the internship-duplicate
  handling noted there) as cards in a responsive grid (1 col mobile, 2 col tablet, 3 col
  desktop), each with title, short description, tech-skill tags, and a live-link button when one
  exists (JemberTrip).
- FR-14: WHEN a project card is hovered (desktop) THE SYSTEM SHALL apply a subtle 3D tilt/parallax
  effect; WHEN tapped (touch), THE SYSTEM SHALL show the same end-state without requiring hover.

### Certifications
- FR-15: THE certifications section SHALL render all 5 entries from 1.6 as badge/cards with
  issuer, issue/expiry dates, credential ID where present, and skills tags.

### Contact
- FR-16: THE contact section SHALL provide a form (name, email, message) with client-side
  validation (required fields, valid email format) and SHALL send via EmailJS per `tech.md`.
- FR-17: WHEN the form submits successfully, THE SYSTEM SHALL show a clear success state and clear
  the form. WHEN it fails, THE SYSTEM SHALL show a clear, friendly error state and SHALL NOT lose
  the user's typed input.
- FR-18: THE contact section SHALL also list direct social/contact links (LinkedIn at minimum;
  GitHub/email once provided).

### Footer
- FR-19: THE footer SHALL repeat key social links, a copyright line, and a "back to top" control.

## 3. Non-functional requirements

- NFR-1 (Responsive): The site SHALL be fully usable and visually correct at all breakpoints
  defined in `structure.md`, with no horizontal scrollbars, overlapping text, or clipped content
  at any of them.
- NFR-2 (Performance): See the performance budget in `tech.md`. The site SHALL remain responsive
  to scroll/input (no jank) on a mid-range Android phone, not just on the developer's machine.
- NFR-3 (Accessibility): WCAG 2.1 AA where feasible — semantic HTML landmarks, alt text on all
  images, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, full keyboard navigability of the
  navbar/menu/form, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support that disables/reduces all non-essential
  motion including the 3D scenes.
- NFR-4 (SEO): Proper `<title>`/meta description, Open Graph + Twitter Card tags, a single `<h1>`,
  logical heading hierarchy, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, and descriptive `alt` text.
- NFR-5 (Cross-browser): Correct rendering in latest Chrome, Firefox, Safari/WebKit (desktop +
  iOS), and Edge.
- NFR-6 (Resilience): If WebGL is unavailable or fails to initialize, THE SYSTEM SHALL fall back to
  a static image/gradient hero instead of a blank or broken section — never let a 3D failure break
  the whole page.
- NFR-7 (No bugs at ship time): See "Definition of done" in `product.md` and "Definition of no
  bugs" in `tech.md` — both must be satisfied before this is considered complete.
